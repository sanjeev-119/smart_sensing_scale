Smart Sensing Scale for Comprehensive Diabetic Foot Monitoring

Project Overview

Abstract

Diabetic foot complication remain a leading cause of preventable hospitalization and lower extremity amputation, primarily due to the lack of early detection and monitoring of foot health. Hence, this project aims to develop a smart sensing scale that integrates pressure and temperature sensor arrays to monitor foot health in diabetic patients. The scale will provide real-time feedback on pressure distribution and temperature variations across different foot zones, enabling early detection of potential risks of DFU.

The system integrates a force-sensitive resistor (FSR) array to map plantar pressure distribution, thermal camera IR array (MLX90640) to detect zonal thermal asymmetry, all interfaced through a Rasberry Pi microcontroller. Acquired data is processed on-device using a threshold-based risk-scoring algorithm that flags peak pressure zones and temperature differentials exceeding clinically significant limits, with results displayed instantly. Readings are additionally transmitted over Wi-Fi to a backend server, enabling historical trend analysis and remote visualization through a web dashboard.

Objectives

  • To design and fabricate a scale-like platform integrating pressure and temperature sensor arrays for foot monitoring.
  • To develop a software for collecting, storing, analyzing, and visualizing the sensor data to identify pressure and temperature variations across different foot zones.
